## End-to-end Reports blueprint — consolidated review draft, 9 October 2026

Central placement and shared source ownership are approved. The complete operating journey below is a **review proposal**, preserving each module's approved metric laws rather than inventing a new six-tab formula. It does not authorise UI/build/export grants or scheduled sending.

1. **Open Reports or a module summary.** Use one central destination. A summary opens the corresponding topic with the same scope/period/filter context; unavailable destination must not masquerade as a live report. Workspace remains today's actions.
2. **Select what and when.** Choose an authorised subject and available period. Report shows the date anchor used, data coverage and as-of time. Period choices longer than stored coverage disclose that limit. Comparison uses the same definition and comparable coverage; incomplete comparison is explicitly unavailable.
3. **Read results with provenance.** Reuse the owning module's arithmetic and source records. Distinguish genuine zero, no records, unreadable source, missing date, incomplete history and a rate withheld for inadequate sample. Source failure never becomes zero. Money visibility follows existing permissions, not a Finance-only RM blanket rule.
4. **Investigate a figure.** Open its source register/object with the same filters. Preserve exact SO/PO/DO/Unit/payment/Case attribution. One SO can have multiple goods, POs, DOs or payments; no joining them into duplicated totals. Report is read-only; corrections use the original module door and historical rules.
5. **Compare and export.** Export the authorised source definition/filter/as-of result; permission and sensitive-field scope remain source-specific. Missing or failed export stays failed, with safe retry and no fabricated success. Proposed generic exports require their own confirmed contract; existing module exports retain their approved scope.
6. **Scheduled report is separate.** Its event/time/frequency/recipients/channel/version and send evidence are separately governed. Evening content approval is not timing or automatic-send approval. No recipient guesses, automatic mailing or mandatory historical imports.

**Every metric definition needs:** unit of analysis, source owner, formula/numerator/denominator, date anchor/calendar/time zone, included/excluded outcomes, missing-data/sample policy, currency/access, refresh/as-of and drill-through. A definition absent from its owning source is proposed, not guessed. Report pages must not introduce new business status or a second calculation engine.

**Acceptance cases:** central and module summary reconcile on identical filters; a multi-PO/multi-DO SO does not multiply order totals; partial delivery preserves remainder; unpaid storage and RM0 goods balance remain distinct; Finance hold is not money; missing actual delivery time prevents minute-level punctuality; failed source differs from zero; restricted user cannot export forbidden fields; cancelled/test/rental exclusions follow the metric's source; historical range is truthful; export retains selected scope; source correction refreshes the same shared result; scheduled report remains off without approved configuration. All are **not executed** by this document pass.

Remaining owner decisions are only definitions not already governed (new Overview/Team measures), exact reporting/navigation grouping, evening timing/recipients, and any new export/sending policy.
