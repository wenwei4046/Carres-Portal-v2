# Carres ERP UI audit, 29 Oct 2026

Method: ui-ux-pro-max priority table (1 accessibility, 2 touch, 5 layout, 8 forms and states, 9 navigation),
a read-only walk of https://erp.carresofficial.com as Sara (Principal) at 1280x900 and 375x812,
a static scan of `apps/web/src` on main `cad175ea4`, and the Houzs ERP frontend code
(`Houzs-ERP/frontend/src`). Houzs prod was not walked: the browser had no Houzs session, and no
password was typed. Dealer, supplier and partner portals redirect a Principal away, so those are code-only.

Source tags: [prod UI] seen on prod, [code: file:line], [my guess].

Carres identity stays: logo, brand colours and the name. We adopt Houzs structure and states, not its branding.
Where this audit differs from `docs/ui/MASTER.md`, the owner's 29 Oct instruction applies ("use them to the max
extent", YH). Most fixes below are what MASTER §2.2 already asks for and the code does not yet do.
