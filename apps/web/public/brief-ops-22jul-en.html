Carres Orders — the new way, in 5 minutes

Team briefing · Wed 22 Jul 26 · 10:30 (Jess leads; we start using it after this)

1 · Your day = the QUEUES on the left, four verbs

Every order's NEXT column tells you the next step. The QUEUES on the left are just the totals of those steps — click one to get the list:

VerbWhat it meansWho does it
Order PO redGoods not yet ordered from the supplierPO duty person
Chase supplier amberPO placed, goods not in the warehouse yet → chase the factoryPO duty person
Assign logisticNo carrier picked yetPIC (your own orders)
Chase logisticCarrier picked, slot not fixed with the customer → chase the carrierPIC (your own orders)

Overdue = the delivery date we promised has passed and it's not delivered (a result). Who to chase is in that row's NEXT. Owing = the customer still owes money (PIC chases).

2 · PIC = your customers (orders are split per person)

  • Every order has one PIC (your avatar chip). Click your own avatar top-right = see only your orders.
  • You chase the money (Owing) and the delivery (logistic); if the customer calls, you answer.
  • Chase the customer / carrier with the system's Chase button → one merged message per counterparty per day. Don't fire off scattered messages.

3 · PO duty (goods bought together) — Jul Shasha · Aug Li Ching · Sep Khor Yee

  • POs are raised company-wide, together — never split per person. Only the duty person (+ management) can press Raise PO.
  • Every Mon / Thu = PO day: the system opens a task for the duty person + a banner on Orders → tick the orders → Raise PO → one card per supplier → Send each.
  • Red urgent orders (deadline inside the stock window: mattress/bedframe 7 days · sofa 5 days) must be raised immediately, any day — don't wait for PO day.
  • Only the duty person is the single voice to a supplier on WhatsApp. Want to know when goods arrive → ask the duty person internally, don't poke the supplier yourself. Month-end handover = the open-PO list.

4 · WhatsApp group rule — one message, one order

  • One message = one order, start with the REF (e.g. CR1187 customer wants Saturday delivery).
  • No message without a REF — questions, updates, anything: put the REF first, or the group can't trace it.
  • Whoever posts owns the thread to the end (your thread, your follow-up); nobody else picks it up.

5 · Automatic rules (you don't manage these, but know them)

  • First login = auto-joins the pool, auto-gets orders. Creating the account is the only manual step.
  • Before 10:00 not logged in — your share stays yours; after 10:00 still not in = the system treats you as absent today, your orders flow to whoever is in; you log in and they flow back. No need to report MC to the system — it knows.
  • The away toggle is only for planned long leave; manual re-assign is Jess / management only.
  • Finished an order → press Mark delivered to clear it. Delivered orders never ring again.
